MathSurface

Interactive 3D math surface generator for 3D printing. Visualize and export z = f(x, y) equations as print-ready STL files.

How to use

  1. Enter your equation in the z = f(x, y) field.
  2. Click Generate or pick a preset example.
  3. Use + Add surface for up to four equations at once. Where two of them cross, the intersection curve is drawn automatically. The colour swatch shows or hides a surface.
  4. Switch to Revolution for the disk, washer and shell methods. The panel compares the exact integral against the volume of the mesh it built, and the Riemann n slider draws the n slices the integral sums.
  5. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
  6. Under Visualization, Underside picks a flat printable base or a constant-thickness shell.
  7. Adjust sliders to tune parameters in real time.
  8. Click Export STL to download for 3D printing.
